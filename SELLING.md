# Selling Autohive — Sell-Kit

This is everything you need to list Autohive and find a buyer **yourself**. No AI
can ethically go find a real buyer for you — that means contacting strangers and
brokering money. What follows is the toolkit so *you* can do it fast and not get
lowballed or scammed.

> ⚠️ **Honesty checkpoint before you list.** This repository contains the
> **landing page only** — no backend, user accounts, billing, or revenue data.
> If you're selling Autohive as a *running business*, every claim below marked
> `[FILL IN]` must be backed by **real, provable numbers** (Stripe dashboard,
> bank statements, analytics). Buyers run due diligence; inflated or invented
> metrics kill deals and can expose you to fraud claims. Sell the truth — the
> truth is sellable.

## 2. Valuation — how buyers will price it

Small SaaS / micro-startups typically sell for a **multiple of annual profit
(SDE)** or a **multiple of MRR/ARR**. Honest current-market ranges:

- **Profit multiple:** ~**2×–4× annual net profit (SDE)** for a small, owner-run
  SaaS. Higher with strong growth, low churn, clean books.
- **Revenue multiple:** ~**2×–5× ARR** if it's growing fast (the higher end
  needs real month-over-month growth, not a flat line).

```
→ Optimistic asking price  (4–5× SDE) .... $[FILL IN]
```

**What moves the multiple UP** (do these before listing if you can):
- 6+ months of clean, exportable revenue history (Stripe/Paddle)
- Low churn (< ~5%/mo) and some MoM growth
- Diversified customers (no single customer = most of revenue)
- Documented, low-maintenance operations (the SOPs in §5)

**What pulls it DOWN:** flat/declining revenue, high churn, heavy founder
dependence, messy or unverifiable books, no real moat.

## 4. Where to list it (real marketplaces)

Pick based on deal size:

| Marketplace | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Acquire.com** | SaaS w/ real MRR | Built for startup acquisitions; vetted buyers, deal tooling. Strong first choice for a running SaaS. |
| **Flippa** | Sites & small businesses | Huge audience, broad buyer pool; more tire-kickers, vet carefully. |
| **MicroAcquire / Tiny / Empire Flippers** | Established, higher-revenue SaaS | Empire Flippers vets listings; good if revenue is solid. |
| **IndieHackers / r/SideProject / X** | Founder-to-founder | Free; post the story, let inbound come to you. |
| **SaaS broker** | $100k+ deals | Takes a commission (~10–15%) but handles buyers + paperwork. |

**Recommended path:** list on **Acquire.com** (primary) + post the story on
**X / IndieHackers** (free inbound). Use the cold email in §6 to nudge 10–20
strategic buyers directly.

## 5. Due-diligence pack (prepare BEFORE you list)

Have these ready — buyers move fast when you do, and stall when you don't:

- [ ] **Financials:** Stripe/Paddle export, 6–12 mo P&L, MRR/churn chart
- [ ] **Traffic/analytics:** GA or Plausible export
- [ ] **Customer list:** counts + plan mix (anonymized until under NDA)
- [ ] **Tech:** repo access, infra/hosting list, monthly running costs, API keys inventory
- [ ] **Ops/SOPs:** how the business is run weekly (so a buyer can take over)
- [ ] **Legal:** domain ownership, trademarks, any contracts/ToS/privacy policy
- [ ] **Transfer plan:** domain, code, Stripe, integrations, + a handover period (e.g. 30 days support)

## 7. Don't get scammed

Selling attracts predators. Hard rules:

- **Never transfer the domain/code before payment clears.** Use **escrow**
  (Escrow.com, or the marketplace's built-in escrow).
- **Verify the buyer** can actually pay before sharing sensitive financials.
- **Watch for overpayment/"wrong amount, refund me the difference" scams** —
  classic fraud. Walk away.
- **Get it in writing:** a simple asset purchase agreement. For larger deals,
  have a lawyer review (~a few hundred $ that saves you thousands).
- **Use NDAs** before handing over the customer list or full books.
