## Description (paste below the line)

---

**A conversion-focused real estate landing page — rebranded and live in 10 minutes.**

Keystone is a modern, dark-themed landing page built for real estate
brokerages, flat-fee startups, and individual agents. It's pure HTML, CSS,
and JavaScript — **no build step, no frameworks, no dependencies**. Unzip it,
open `index.html`, and it works.

**What's on the page**

- 🏠 Hero with email lead capture + animated "live deal" dashboard mockup
- 📊 Stats band (savings, rebate, time-to-offer)
- 🧩 6-card services grid (flat-fee selling, smart search, buyer rebate, photos/3D tours, valuation, closing help)
- 👣 3-step "How it works"
- ⚖️ "Traditional way vs. you" comparison section
- 💰 3-tier pricing section with a featured plan
- ❓ FAQ accordion (native `<details>` — zero JS needed)
- ✉️ Final CTA with a second lead-capture form

**Built to convert, built to ship**

- ✅ Working email validation + demo mode (signups saved in the browser until you connect a backend)
- ✅ **Formspree-ready** — paste one form ID and you're collecting real leads (free tier works)
- ✅ Fully responsive: desktop, tablet, mobile
- ✅ `prefers-reduced-motion` support, semantic HTML, ARIA labels
- ✅ SEO meta + Open Graph social-sharing tags included
- ✅ ~35 KB total. Loads instantly. 3 files.

**Rebrand in 10 minutes (guide included)**

- Colors = **2 CSS variables**
- Name = one find-and-replace
- Step-by-step `SETUP.md` covers rebranding, connecting the form, custom
  domain, and free deployment (GitHub Pages / Netlify / Vercel)

**What you get**

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`
- `SETUP.md` — the 10-minute rebrand & launch guide
- Desktop + mobile screenshots
- Simple commercial license: use it for unlimited personal & client
  projects. (No reselling the template itself.)

*Placeholder stats and testimonial-style numbers on the page are sample copy —
replace them with your real figures before going live.*
