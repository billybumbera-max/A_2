# Direct-to-realtor outreach kit

The biggest single payout: sell this page to ONE agent or small brokerage,
rebranded with their name, for **$500–$750**. Agents routinely pay agencies
$1,500+ for worse.

## Pricing script

| Package | Price | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **Page only** | $500 | Page rebranded with their name/colors/photo, lead form connected to their email, deployed free (Netlify/GitHub Pages) |
| **Page + domain & launch** | $750 | Everything above + domain purchase/setup (theirs), Google Analytics, submitted to Google Search Console |

Deliver in 48 hours. Half up front, half on delivery — or full payment up
front with a 100% refund guarantee if they don't love it.

## Where to find agents (in order of reply rate)

1. **Instagram** — search `#realtor` + your city; agents with 1–10K followers
   and a Linktree but no real site are the sweet spot
2. **Facebook** — local "Real Estate Agents of [City]" groups
3. **Zillow agent directory** — filter your city, they list emails/phones
4. **LinkedIn** — search "realtor" + city, filter 2nd connections

Send 20–30 messages. Expect 3–6 replies, 1–2 closes.

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I already have a website" | "This isn't a website replacement — it's a *campaign* page. One focused page for your ads/QR codes/bio link that captures leads, instead of sending people to a homepage with 40 links." |
| "My brokerage gives me a page" | "Those template pages promote the brokerage, not you. This one is 100% your brand, your leads, your list." |
| "$500 is a lot" | "One closed lead pays for it 20×. And there's no monthly fee — you own it outright." |
| "Can it do IDX/MLS listings?" | "It's a lead-capture page, not a portal — that's why it converts. I link your MLS/Zillow profile for inventory." |
