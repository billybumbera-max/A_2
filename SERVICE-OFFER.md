# AgentPilot — Service Offer (real estate AI follow-up)

This is what you actually sell. Use it as your pitch script, your proposal,
and the source of truth for what's included.

## The offer in one sentence
> "I set up an AI assistant that replies to every one of your leads in under
> 60 seconds, follows up until they book, and schedules showings for you —
> done-for-you in 3 days."

## Who it's for
Real estate agents and small teams who **buy online leads** (Zillow,
Realtor.com, Facebook, portals) and lose deals because they can't reply fast
enough or follow up consistently.

## The problem you solve (say it back to them)
- Online buyers go with **whoever replies first** — and the average agent
  takes hours.
- Deals are won on the **5th–8th follow-up**, but most agents stop after one.
- Nights, weekends, and showings = missed inquiries.
- That's **paid leads going to waste.** One lost deal is €5k–€15k+ in
  commission.

## What you deliver
| Component | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Instant lead responder** | Auto-replies to every new lead in seconds, in their voice |
| **Follow-up sequences** | Multi-touch follow-ups until the lead books or opts out |
| **Calendar booking** | Qualifies + books showings straight to their calendar |
| **CRM sync** | Logs every conversation and status automatically |
| **Review/referral asks** | Requests reviews from closed clients at the right moment |
| **Setup + training** | You connect everything and tune the voice. Client does nothing technical. |

## Pricing (anchor high, justify with ROI)
| Package | Setup (one-time) | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Solo Agent | €499 | €149 |
| **Producer** (most pick this) | **€1,500** | **€299** |
| Team / Brokerage | Custom (€3k–€8k) | Custom |

**ROI line to use:** "Your average commission is €X. If this saves *one* deal
a year, it's paid for many times over. Most clients see that in the first
month."

## Why now / why you
- AI makes instant, personalized follow-up possible for the first time.
- Done-for-you — they don't touch software.
- Live in 3 days, not months.

## How you actually fulfill it (your cost is mostly time)
Stack you can assemble per client: a lead-capture webhook → an AI responder
(e.g. an LLM API) → their calendar + CRM via a no-code automation tool
(Make/Zapier/n8n). The €1,500 is for the **setup + judgment**, not the
software. Your hard costs are low; your margin is your time.

## The math toward a big number
- 1 Producer client = €1,500 setup + €299/mo = **~€5,088 in year one.**
- 20 clients = **~€100k/year recurring-ish.**
- The monthly fees stack: the more clients you keep, the less new selling you
  need. That recurring base is what scales toward life-changing numbers —
  not any single sale.

## Objection handling
- *"Too expensive."* → "What's one lost deal worth to you? This stops you
  losing them."
- *"Will it sound robotic?"* → "It speaks in your voice and hands off to you
  instantly when needed. You approve everything first."
- *"I already follow up."* → "In under 60 seconds, every time, 24/7, on the
  8th touch? This is the version of you that never sleeps or forgets."
- *"Is my data safe?"* → "Secure connections, you stay in control, nothing
  sent without your approved rules."
