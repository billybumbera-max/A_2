# Outreach kit — getting your first AgentPilot clients

The product doesn't make money. **Outreach does.** Goal: book setup calls.
Aim for ~30 quality contacts/day. Expect ~1–3 calls per 30, and ~1 client per
3–5 calls early on. So roughly **30–150 contacts → 1 client**, improving fast
as you refine.

---

## Step 1 — Build a target list (1 hour)
Find agents who *buy online leads* (they have budget and the exact pain):
- **Zillow** "Premier Agent" listings in a city → they pay for leads.
- **Realtor.com** featured agents.
- **Facebook/Instagram** — agents running lead-gen ads (check the Meta Ad
  Library for "[city] homes for sale").
- Local brokerages' agent directories.

Put 100+ into a sheet: name, brokerage, email, Instagram/FB, city. (Tip: the
included `SETUP-GOOGLE-SHEET.md` flow works great as your lead tracker too.)

## Step 2 — Send the message
Lead with *their* pain, not your tech. Keep it short. Personalize line 1.

### Cold email
**Subject:** quick question about your Zillow leads

> Hi {FirstName},
>
> I saw you're active in {City} real estate — are you happy with how fast your
> online leads get a reply right now?
>
> I set agents up with an AI assistant that replies to every new lead in
> under 60 seconds, follows up until they book a showing, and puts it on your
> calendar — all in your voice, done-for-you in about 3 days.
>
> Most agents I talk to are losing deals just to slow follow-up. Worth a quick
> 15-min call to see if it'd help you? Here's a 2-minute look: {YOUR_DEMO_URL}
>
> — {YourName}

### Instagram / Facebook DM
> Hey {FirstName}! Love your {City} listings. Quick one — when a new online
> lead comes in after hours, how fast do they hear back? I build AI assistants
> for agents that reply in <60s and book the showing automatically. Mind if I
> send a 2-min demo?

### Follow-up #1 (day 3, reply to your own email)
> Hi {FirstName} — bumping this in case it got buried. The agents who win
> online leads are just the ones who reply first. Happy to show you how it'd
> work for your pipeline — 15 min this week?

### Follow-up #2 (day 7)
> Last nudge, {FirstName} — I'll close your spot for this month otherwise.
> Even if it's not now, here's the demo so it's on your radar: {YOUR_DEMO_URL}

## Step 3 — On the call (15–20 min)
1. **Ask, don't pitch:** "Walk me through what happens when a new lead comes
   in at 9pm." (Let them feel the gap.)
2. **Quantify the pain:** "What's a typical commission for you? How many
   online leads a month?" → the lost-deal math sells itself.
3. **Show the demo** ({YOUR_DEMO_URL}) and the relevant outcomes.
4. **Present Producer (€1,500 + €299/mo)** as the default. Anchor there.
5. **Close:** "I can have you live in 3 days. Want me to get started?"
6. Take a deposit to lock the build.

## Step 4 — Deliver & get referrals
- Deliver in 3 days. Over-communicate.
- After 2 weeks: "Happy with how it's going? Who's one other agent who'd want
  this?" Referrals are your cheapest channel.

---

## Daily routine (your first 2 weeks)
- **30 new contacts** (email + DM).
- **All due follow-ups.**
- **Every booked call** prepped with their name/city/brokerage.
- Track everything in your sheet. Conversion improves as you tighten the pitch.

## Channels, ranked
1. **Instagram/FB DMs** — agents live there; high reply rate.
2. **Cold email** — scalable; use the templates above.
3. **Referrals** — best quality once you have 1–2 happy clients.
4. **Local meetups / brokerage offices** — warm, high-trust, slower.

> Reality check: the first 5 clients are the hardest. Once you have proof and
> referrals, it compounds. Volume of outreach early = speed to revenue.
