## ⚠️ Realistic pricing — read this first

What this asset is worth depends entirely on **how you sell it**:

| Sell it as… | Realistic price | Effort | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reusable HTML template** (sell many times) | **€19–€59** each | List once, passive | ThemeForest, Gumroad, Creative Market |
| **Exclusive template** (sell once, buyer owns it) | **€150–€600** | One-time | Flippa "starter sites", Gumroad |
| **Custom build for a real business** | **€800–€3,000+** | Find 1 client, tweak branding | Upwork, Fiverr Pro, cold outreach, local businesses |
| **Established site w/ traffic + signups** | **€3,000+** | Months of growth first | Flippa, Acquire.com, Empire Flippers |

**Honest take on €3,000:** a brand-new landing page with no traffic, no
revenue, and no users does **not** sell for €3,000 as-is — buyers on site
marketplaces pay for *proven* traffic/revenue. The fastest realistic route to
€3,000 is the **"custom build for a client"** row: position yourself as
*"I build AI-automation landing pages for startups, €X each"* and sell the
**work**, using this as your portfolio piece. One client at €1.5–3k beats a
hundred €20 template sales.

## 🛒 Where to list it (ranked)

1. **Gumroad** (gumroad.com) — easiest, takes ~10% + fees, instant payouts,
   no approval. Best for selling the template repeatedly. Start here.
2. **ThemeForest / Envato** (themeforest.net) — biggest template marketplace,
   but has a quality review + exclusivity rules. Higher trust, slower start.
3. **Creative Market** (creativemarket.com) — design-buyer audience, simple
   upload.
4. **Flippa** (flippa.com) — for selling it as a one-off "starter site"
   /exclusive asset (the €150–600 row).
5. **Upwork / Fiverr** — don't sell the file; sell *"I'll build your startup
   landing page."* This is the €800–3,000 path.

## 💡 The €3,000 game plan (realistic)
1. Deploy this as your **demo/portfolio**.
2. Pick a niche (AI automation agencies, local service businesses going
   "AI-powered", indie SaaS founders).
3. Reach out to 20–30 of them: *"I build conversion landing pages with a
   working waitlist, live in 3 days, €1,500."* Show the demo.
4. Close one. Re-skin this template to their brand. Deliver. Invoice.
5. Repeat. The template is your leverage; the **service** is the €3k.
