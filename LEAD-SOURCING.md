# Building your 100 lead-buying agents (the right way)

Goal: a real list of agents who **pay for online leads** (they have budget +
the exact pain AgentPilot fixes). Do this manually/legitimately — quality
beats a scraped junk list, and it keeps you out of trouble (see Compliance).

Import `leads-tracker.csv` into Google Sheets and fill it as you go.

## Where to find them (pick 1–2 cities, go deep)
1. **Zillow → "Find an Agent"** for your city. Agents with the **"Premier
   Agent"** badge are literally paying Zillow for leads. Note name + brokerage.
2. **Realtor.com → "Find Realtors"** — "Featured"/advertising agents = lead buyers.
3. **Meta Ad Library** (facebook.com/ads/library) → search *"[city] homes for
   sale"* / *"free home valuation"*. Anyone running these ads is buying leads
   and feeling the follow-up pain hard. **Best signal on the list.**
4. **Instagram:** search `#[city]realtor`, `#[city]realestate`. Active posters
   with "DM me" / Linktree = reachable and online.
5. **Local brokerage sites** (Keller Williams, RE/MAX, eXp offices) → agent
   directories with public business contact info.

## What to capture per agent (columns in the tracker)
Name · Brokerage · City · Business email · Instagram/FB · Phone (if public) ·
Source · Buys-leads signal (e.g. "Zillow Premier" / "running FB ads") · Status.

## How to find a business email (legit)
- Their personal website / brokerage profile "Contact" page.
- Their Instagram bio / link.
- Public business listings. Prefer **business** addresses over scraped personal
  ones. If only a contact form or DM exists, use that channel instead.

## Speed tips
- Batch by source: one sitting on Zillow, one on the Ad Library, etc.
- 100 is ~2–3 focused hours. Don't over-research — name, channel, signal, next.
- Prioritize the **Meta Ad Library** finds first; they convert best.

## Daily flow (pairs with OUTREACH.md)
- Top up the list to keep ~50 "not yet contacted".
- Contact 30/day, log every touch + the next follow-up date.
- Move warm replies to "Call booked".

## ⚠️ Compliance (you're in the EU — read this)
Cold outreach is legal but regulated. Stay clean:
- **B2B is the safe lane.** Contact agents at their **business** address about a
  **business** offer relevant to them — that's the defensible basis. Avoid
  scraping/buying personal contact lists.
- **Every email must include:** who you are, a real reason you're contacting
  *them*, and an easy way to opt out ("Reply 'stop' and I won't contact you
  again"). Honor opt-outs immediately and permanently.
- **Don't spoof, don't mislead** subject lines, don't hide your identity.
- **DMs:** follow each platform's rules; don't blast identical messages so fast
  you get flagged.
- If unsure about a specific country's rules, check before scaling volume.

> Not legal advice — but these habits keep cold B2B outreach low-risk.
