What does Autohive actually do?

Autohive runs AI agents that take over repetitive, rules-light work — triaging your inbox, entering data, generating reports, sending follow-ups — by understanding context and acting across the tools you already use.

Do I need to be technical to use it?

No. You connect your tools with a few clicks and describe what you want done in plain English. Autohive builds and runs the workflow for you.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We connect via secure OAuth and never store your passwords. You set guardrails and approval steps, so a human stays in control of anything sensitive.
