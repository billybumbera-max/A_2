Questions, answered

Everything you might be wondering before you join.

How is Keystone cheaper than a normal agent?

Traditional agents typically charge around 6% of your sale price. Keystone agents are salaried experts, so you pay one low flat listing fee instead — and buyers get 1% of the purchase price back at closing.

Are these real, licensed agents?

Yes. Every Keystone agent is a licensed, experienced local professional. The tech makes them faster and more responsive — it doesn't replace the human guiding your deal.

Where is Keystone available?

We're live in select metros and expanding fast. Join the waitlist with your email and we'll let you know the moment we're in your area.

What does the buyer rebate actually mean?

When you buy a home with a Keystone agent, we share part of our commission with you — 1% of the purchase price, paid back at closing toward your costs. On a $500K home, that's $5,000.

What does it cost to get started?

Nothing. Getting matched with an agent and exploring your options is free. You only pay the flat listing fee if and when your home sells.
