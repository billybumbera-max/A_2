# AI Automation Agency — Starter Kit 📦

Everything you need to launch an AI-automation services business, in one
package. Built to be sold as a digital product **or** used by you to land
your own clients.

> **The idea:** you don't sell software — you sell *done-for-you AI
> automation* to businesses, using these assets as your demo, your pitch, and
> your playbook.

## What's in the box

| File / folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `gallery.html` | **Showcase page** linking all 6 templates — your demo hub. |
| `index.html` + `styles.css` + `script.js` | **Autohive** — a generic AI-automation landing page with a working waitlist. Your flagship demo. |
| `realestate/` | **AgentPilot** — AI lead follow-up for real estate agents. |

| `screenshots/` | Ready-made preview images for listings and pitches. |

## Two ways to make money with it

**A) Run the agency (higher ceiling).**
Use the pages as demos. Follow `SERVICE-OFFER.md` + `OUTREACH.md` to land
clients at €499–€1,500 setup + €149–€299/mo. The recurring fees compound.

**B) Sell the kit as a product (passive).**
List this whole package on Gumroad/marketplaces per `SALES-KIT.md`. Price it
€29–€99. Each sale is one upload's worth of effort.

## Fastest start (your checklist)
1. Deploy the pages for live demos (free — see `README.md`).
2. Pick a niche (real estate is pre-built; clone it for others via `CUSTOMIZE.md`).
3. Build a 50–100 prospect list (`LEAD-SOURCING.md` → `leads-tracker.csv`).
4. Send 20–30 outreach messages/day (`OUTREACH.md`). Book calls. Close one.
5. Deliver, get referrals, repeat. Reinvest into reaching more of the niche.
